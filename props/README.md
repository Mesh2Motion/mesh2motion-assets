# Props

Props (weapons, tools, and other held items) used with the Mesh2Motion animations.

## Sources and credits

These props are not original Mesh2Motion artwork. They come from two free CC0 (public domain) asset creators:

- **KayKit** by Kay Lousberg: the weapons come from the *KayKit Fantasy Weapons Bits* pack (this is also where `weapons_bits_texture.png` comes from).
  - Website: https://kaylousberg.com/
  - Asset store: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/
- **Quaternius**: low-poly models from the Quaternius packs.
  - Website: https://quaternius.com/
  - Patreon: https://www.patreon.com/quaternius

Both are released under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so no attribution is required, but please consider supporting the creators if you find their work useful.
